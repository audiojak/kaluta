Direction 1: Paper

A reading room. Mail is mostly reading and writing, so the app looks like a well-set page: a serif for subjects and headlines, warm paper instead of white, ink instead of black, hairlines instead of boxes, and one deep green for anything Kaluta does. Quiet enough that the mail is the loudest thing on screen.

Gains

  • Unmistakable at a glance without a single custom control: type and colour carry it
  • Makes long mail pleasant to read, which is where the time goes
  • Ages well; nothing here is a trend

Loses

  • A serif in lists can feel bookish to people who want a tool
  • Warm paper fights with HTML mail designed for white: the reader needs a white card for those
  • Furthest from the platform's look, so system surfaces (sheets, menus) will contrast with it
